Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Lettertide is a place for kids and grown-ups to write letters to their future selves, painted postcards to people they love, and letters to Santa. Letters are personal, so we collect as little as we can and explain all of it here in plain language.

The short version

  • Right now, your letters are saved only on your own device. We can’t see them.
  • We never contact the people you write postcards to, and we never collect their names, numbers or emails. You send postcards yourself, from your own phone.
  • No ads. We never sell your information or share it for marketing.
  • We count visits without cookies and without collecting who you are, and never what you write.
  • Kids don’t need an account. When email delivery is on, a grown-up signs in.

Your letters

Today: everything you write (letters, time capsules, postcards and letters to Santa) is stored in your browser on the device you used. It is not sent to us. If you clear your browser’s data or use a private window, those letters are deleted and we can’t get them back.

When email delivery is switched on: to send a future letter to your inbox on the right day, Lettertide will store your letters on our server. A grown-up signs in with an email address (we email a one-time sign-in link; there are no passwords). We then store:

  • the grown-up’s email address, and an optional backup contact email if you add one;
  • your letters, time capsules and postcards, and the dates they should arrive;
  • basic records needed to keep your account working, like when you signed in.

Letters are stored with our database provider (Turso) and emails are sent through a Gmail account (Google). Secret-word letters are locked in your browser before they are saved, so nobody, including us, can read them without the word.

Postcards

Postcards are painted on your device. When you tap Send postcard, your phone’s own share menu opens and you choose how to send it (Messages, WhatsApp, email or anything else). The picture goes from your account to theirs. Lettertide never receives or stores the recipient’s contact details. If you schedule a postcard for a special day, we only remind you to send it.

Letters to Santa

Santa’s replies are written on your device from a set of ready-made letters. In the future, replies may be written by an AI service (Claude, made by Anthropic). If so, only the child’s first name, age group and letter are sent to write the reply, and Anthropic’s commercial terms say it does not use this data to train its models. Santa is built to never ask for an address, school, photos, phone number or last name, never suggest keeping secrets from grown-ups, and to encourage a child who mentions feeling unsafe to talk to a trusted grown-up.

Talk instead of typing, and Read it to me

These use the speech features built into your browser. When you talk, your browser’s maker (for example Apple or Google) turns your voice into words under their own privacy policy. Lettertide never receives or stores any audio. Reading a letter aloud happens on your device.

Visit counts

We use GoatCounter to count visits. It uses no cookies and doesn’t track you across websites. It records which pages are opened, simple moments like “a postcard was sent” or “a letter to Santa was written”, the website you came from, your country, and your browser and screen size. Your IP address is used to work out the country and is not stored. What you write is never included.

Other services the website uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, it may keep short-term logs (such as IP addresses) for security.
  • Google Fonts provides the lettering. Your browser downloads the fonts from Google, which can see your IP address.

Children’s privacy

Lettertide is made for children to use with a parent, guardian or teacher nearby. Children don’t create accounts, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. When email delivery is on, the sign-in and email address belong to the grown-up. Please help young children avoid putting things like their full name, address or school in a letter. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Delete any letter from My mailbox at any time.
  • When accounts are on, you can download all of your letters, or delete your account and everything in it, from My mailbox.
  • Email us to ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted.

Keeping things safe

The website uses secure connections (HTTPS), sign-in links expire after 20 minutes and work only once, and we limit how many emails and replies can be sent to prevent misuse. No system is perfect, so please don’t put anything in a letter that would cause harm if someone else saw it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change affects letters you’ve already stored with us, we’ll let you know by email first.
